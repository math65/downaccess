## DownAccess 0.2.0

The biggest update since DownAccess began. Five new features, a complete overhaul of Arte, and two months of user feedback.

### Your files finally know what they contain

Every download now arrives with its title, author, year, genre and cover art. Your player announces them, your library sorts them, your chapters stay in place. A file is no longer a name lost in a folder.

### One file per chapter. Or just the part you care about.

A three-hour lecture, a concert, a show cut into segments: DownAccess can turn it into as many files as there are chapters, each with its own title. Or keep none of them, if you would rather have a single file. The choice is yours, in Preferences.

And when only a few minutes matter, **Download an extract** (Ctrl+E) fetches those alone. You give a start and an end, DownAccess handles the rest.

### The transcript, to read or to keep

When a video has subtitles, you can read its text without downloading it. DownAccess gathers the subtitles into one continuous text, laid out in paragraphs, free of timestamps and repetitions. Read it on screen, copy it, or save it.

### Subscribe. Then forget about it.

Following a show meant remembering to, reopening the page, and comparing from memory with what you had already taken. Not any more.

Subscribe once to a channel, a podcast or an Arte collection (Ctrl+B). At every launch, DownAccess quietly checks what has arrived and shows it to you. You tick what you want — or you let the application download it on its own. A check costs a few kilobytes: nothing slows down at startup, even with dozens of subscriptions.

Just discovered a channel and want to catch up on its past? A tick box offers that as you subscribe.

A **Subscriptions** tab in Preferences settles the rest: check at startup or not, at most once a day, open the new items window straight away or keep the quiet counter, announce new items out loud, and the format offered for your next subscriptions.

### Arte, rebuilt from the ground up

Arte's concerts and live performances were out of reach from DownAccess. They now have their own category, with more than two hundred programmes: festivals, recordings, music. Follow a festival like a channel, and every concert put online is brought to you.

Arte's other catalogues opened up too. Pop culture and Children showed only ten programmes each; they now offer ninety. Documentaries, Films, Science and History have each gained around a hundred entries. And moving between pages of results is now instant.

Opening the page of a festival or a series finally shows the titles of the programmes, their duration and their summary, instead of unreadable web addresses.

### Fixes

- **Several programmes from the same Arte show.** The concerts of a festival all shared one file name: only one downloaded, the others reported "already downloaded". Each programme now gets its own file, named as it appears in the search results. Thanks to Véronique.

- **M6 says how things stand.** M6 catch-up programmes arrived as audio files, with no explanation. The channel locks the picture and lets only the soundtrack through. DownAccess now tells you clearly, before downloading, rather than leaving you with an unusable file. Thanks to Véronique.

- **The MP4 format works on Arte.** Choosing it failed every time on that site, on a technical message.

- **A dropped connection no longer stops everything.** One second of lost Internet while a page was being read was enough to fail the download. DownAccess tries again, and speaks plainly when the connection does not come back.

- **An extract is no longer reported as already downloaded** when it has just been created.

### Note

DRM-protected content (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, M6 catch-up) is not supported. That protection is put in place by the platforms: no application can get around it.
