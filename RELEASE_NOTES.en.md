## DownAccess 0.2.3

### Your series in French, not in the original version

An American series shown in France comes with two soundtracks: the French version and the original one. They have exactly the same quality, and DownAccess took the second. You got your episodes in English, with nothing to warn you before listening.

DownAccess now picks the track matching the language of the application: French when your interface is in French. Videos offering a single track — the vast majority — behave exactly as before.

If it is the original version you want, it stays within reach: the **Choose the format manually...** window now shows a **Language** column, which lets you point at the exact track. Until now, the two tracks of a dubbed series were strictly indistinguishable there.

Thanks to Véronique.

### Note

DRM-protected content (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video) is not supported. That protection is put in place by the platforms: no application can work around it. On M6, only the soundtrack escapes the lock.
